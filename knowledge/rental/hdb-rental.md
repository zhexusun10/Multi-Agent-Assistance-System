# HDB rentals and tenant eligibility

## A six-month minimum and prior approval
HDB bedroom rental has a minimum period of six months per tenant per application. Owners must obtain HDB approval before renting bedrooms, with the corresponding procedures for renewals and new tenants. The private-home three-month minimum does not make a three-month HDB room rental acceptable. Six months is a minimum rule, not a guarantee that any flat is immediately available for six months; check the applicable approval and agreement.

## Foreign students and pass conditions
HDB bedroom tenants must meet the specified identity and lawful-residence conditions. Eligible foreign tenants may hold Student’s Passes, Employment Passes or other qualifying passes; a pass generally needs at least six months’ validity at application. Tourist status cannot simply be treated as qualifying long-term tenancy status. Eligibility also depends on pass categories and other restrictions. Check HDB’s latest terms rather than deciding from “I am a student” or “I have a visa” alone.

## Bedroom rental differs from whole-flat rental
For HDB bedroom rental, owners and authorised occupiers must continue physically living in the flat. Locking one room while living elsewhere does not satisfy the residence requirement. Tenants cannot sublet the bedroom. This rule concerns bedroom rental; do not apply it to every HDB whole-flat rental or claim all tenants must live with the landlord. Whole-flat rentals require separate checks of their rules and approval.

## Preparing an enquiry in this project
Project guidance: ask whether the offer is a whole flat or bedroom, whether the corresponding rental approval can be supplied, how permitted occupants and their names are registered, and whether your pass validity qualifies. Collect only necessary information. Do not ask users to paste passport photos, FIN or full identity numbers into ordinary chat. The system lists checks but does not approve eligibility for HDB.

## Keep user requirements separate from rules
No live-in landlord is a user preference or hard requirement. For an HDB room, explain its possible incompatibility with the continuing-residence rule and ask whether the user would consider a whole flat or private home. Do not silently remove the preference or interpret separate access as proof the owner lives elsewhere. Agent replies and official approvals are different sources; preserve their sources and dates.
